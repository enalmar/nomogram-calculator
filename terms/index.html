What this is

Nomogram is an information and calculation tool. Nothing in this service — including tax calculations, marginal rate figures, scenario comparisons or allowance information — constitutes regulated financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, or a personal recommendation. Nomogram is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and does not carry on any regulated activity. All outputs are generated from assumptions and data you provide and will change if those inputs change. Tax rules and allowances change and depend on your individual circumstances. Before taking, or refraining from taking, any action you should obtain advice from a professional authorised to give it. Free impartial guidance is available from MoneyHelper (moneyhelper.org.uk).

Accuracy

The tax parameters carry the date they were last checked against gov.uk, shown in the footer and beneath the breakdown. Every figure is computed from published rates and thresholds for the tax year you select. Some tax years hold announced measures that are not yet in force, and those are labelled as such. Errors are possible; the figures are illustrative and are not a substitute for a calculation from HMRC or from a qualified adviser.

Use

The site is provided as it is, without warranty. It is free to use, and you may link to any result. No liability is accepted for any decision taken in reliance on the figures it produces.

Availability

The service may change or be withdrawn at any time.
